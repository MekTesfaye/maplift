# Image credits

## Hero photograph

The hero photograph used on the site (`images/hero-homeowner-640.webp` and `images/hero-homeowner-864.webp`) was generated for MapLift. It is not a stock photograph and does not come from Unsplash or Pexels.

It shows a homeowner in the driveway of a suburban Gold Coast-style house, looking at a phone. The phone screen is a blank glow, with no maps, app interface, logos or business names.

A second generated candidate, of a man on a suburban lawn with a jacaranda, was kept for review as `/workspace/maplift-shots/hero-photo-alt.jpg` and is not used on the site.

Free stock options from Pexels and Unsplash were considered first. None combined a bright Australian suburban home, a person clearly using a phone, and a screen with no brand UI or maps, so a generated image was used instead.
