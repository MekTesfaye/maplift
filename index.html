The problem

You can be busy, and still be hard to find

Plenty of good trades on the Gold Coast do the work, then lose the next job because their Google Business Profile is thin. People search on their phones, often only a few kilometres from your base. If Google does not have a clear, complete, well-reviewed listing for you, you may not appear. The call goes to whoever does.

Invisible a few kilometres away

Your service area might cover half the coast. Your listing might still fail to show in the next suburb.

Too few reviews

Plenty of people check reviews before they call. A quiet profile can make you look untested, even if you have been trading for years.

A half-finished profile

Missing categories, hours, services, photos or a clear description. Google has less to work with, and customers are less sure you are the right call.

What we do

We make your Google presence easier to find, and easier to trust

MapLift is a local visibility service for Gold Coast trades. We work on the listing, the reviews, the directories and the small website details that help customers choose you.

  • Google Business Profile optimisation

    Categories, services, description, photos, hours and attributes set up properly, so the listing is complete and consistent.

  • A review system that follows the rules

    Requests and replies that comply with ACCC and Australian Consumer Law rules. We do not write, buy or post fake reviews.

  • Weekly posts and photos

    Regular updates so the profile stays active and looks like a business that is still trading.

  • Website basics

    A simple, clear site, or repairs to the one you have, so clicks from Maps have somewhere useful to go.

  • Citations on Australian directories

    Consistent name, address and phone on listings such as Yellow Pages, hipages, True Local, Oneflare, Apple Business Connect and Bing Places.

  • Local links

    Sensible mentions and links from relevant local sources, not a pile of junk directories.

  • Showing up in AI search

    Consistent business details across Google, directories and your site, so AI assistants that look up local businesses are more likely to find accurate information about you.

FAQs

Common questions

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Monthly care is month to month. We ask for 30 days’ notice if you want to stop, so any scheduled work can be wound down cleanly.

How long do results take?

It varies. Some listings improve within a few weeks. Others take a few months. It depends on your trade, your area, and how complete the profile is today. We do not guarantee rankings, leads or a set number of calls.

Do I need a website?

No, not always. A strong Google Business Profile can work on its own. A simple, clear website does help, and we can sort the basics if you need that.

Do you use fake reviews?

No. We do not write, buy, trade or post fake reviews, and we do not ask customers to leave a review only if it is positive. Review work follows ACCC and Australian Consumer Law rules.

What is the free Maps visibility check?

A short, no-obligation look at how your business appears on Google Maps. You get plain notes on what is missing or holding the listing back. It is not a full audit.

Who is this for?

Gold Coast service trades, in particular aircon, electricians, plumbers and cleaners. If you are in a related trade on the Gold Coast, email us and we will say if we can help.

Can you guarantee I will be number one on Maps?

No. No honest service can. We work on the parts of your listing and local presence that you can control. We will be straight with you about what we cannot control.
